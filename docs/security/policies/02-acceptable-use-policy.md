## 1. Purpose

Define how TruckFixr personnel may use company systems, accounts, and data.

## 2. Applies to

All employees, founders, and contractors with access to TruckFixr systems or data.

## 3. Rules

- Use company accounts and access only for legitimate TruckFixr work.
- Protect credentials; never share passwords; enable MFA where available.
- Keep customer personal data (driver emails, phones, VINs, inspection photos) on
  approved systems only — do not copy it to personal devices, personal cloud
  storage, spreadsheets, or chat tools.
- Do not paste customer personal data or secrets into third-party tools (including
  AI assistants) except the approved product integrations that already process it.
- Devices used for TruckFixr work must have disk encryption and a screen lock
  enabled, and run a supported, patched OS.
- Report lost/stolen devices and suspected security incidents immediately per the
  [Incident Response Plan](05-incident-response-plan.md).

## 4. Prohibited

- Attempting to access data outside your authorization (including other fleets).
- Disabling or bypassing security controls.
- Installing unvetted software that handles customer data.

## 5. Enforcement

Violations may result in revoked access and disciplinary action. Report concerns
to the Security Lead.
