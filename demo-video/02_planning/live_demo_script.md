# Live Demo Script — TruckFixr Fleet AI (coolant-leak workflow)

A talk-track for delivering this demo live (sales call, pilot meeting, event booth). ~4–6 min with
pauses for questions. Mirrors the video so the story stays consistent. Keep claims disciplined:
"helps / supports / AI-assisted / built with a path to." Never "predicts / fully automates / replaces."

## 0. Set the scene (30s)
> "Before I click anything — think about how a coolant leak gets reported in most fleets today. A
> call, a text, a paper inspection, a photo in a group chat. The team's working hard, but the
> information you need to make the maintenance decision ends up scattered. That delay is where a
> small issue turns into downtime. Let me show you how TruckFixr closes that gap."

## 1. Dashboard (45s)
- Open the fleet dashboard.
> "This is the manager's view — vehicles, open issues, inspection activity, and maintenance
> priorities in one place instead of across tools."
- (If demoing on seeded/populated data, point to an open item. On a clean account, say: "In a live
  fleet this fills with your vehicles and open defects.")

## 2. Driver reports the coolant leak (60s)
- Switch to the mobile/PWA driver view. Select the unit.
> "Here's the driver at pre-trip. They get a low-coolant warning and see coolant near the reservoir.
> Instead of a loose text, they report it straight into TruckFixr."
- Start the daily inspection / report the defect → pick the cooling/fluid-leak item → add the note →
  attach a photo → submit.
> "Vehicle, symptom, note, and a photo — captured together as a structured record the manager can
> act on."

## 3. AI-assisted triage (45s)
> "TruckFixr organizes that report and runs AI-assisted triage. It flags this high priority —
> a coolant leak can cause overheating if the truck is dispatched without inspection — and suggests
> next steps: check the reservoir, cap, hoses and clamps, and pressure-test the cooling system."
- **Honesty note:** it's decision *support*, not automated diagnosis. If asked: "It helps the team
  prioritize and gives a starting point — the mechanic still makes the call."

## 4. History + compliance (45s)
- Open the vehicle record.
> "Before deciding, the manager sees the vehicle's history, recent maintenance, inspection status,
> and upcoming compliance — all in one record. The issue stops being a message and becomes part of
> the vehicle's history."

## 5. The decision (30s)
> "Now the call is easy and fast: hold the truck, schedule the repair, and assign a backup unit —
> before dispatch, before it becomes a roadside problem."
- If scheduling/backup-unit isn't a built action in the account: "Today that's captured as the
  manager's decision and note; it's on our roadmap as a scheduled action."

## 6. Close — pilot / partner (30s)
> "That's the whole loop: driver report, AI-assisted triage, history and compliance, and a faster
> maintenance decision. TruckFixr is live and we're running pilots with small and mid-sized fleets.
> It's also built with a path to ELD, telematics, fault-code, and repair-partner integrations. Want
> to talk about a pilot for your fleet?"
- Leave-behind: truckfixr.com · 905-677-7663 · this demo video link.

## Q&A quick answers (claims-safe)
- **"Does it diagnose automatically?"** → "It's AI-*assisted* — it prioritizes and suggests next
  steps; your team decides."
- **"Does it integrate with my ELD?"** → "We're built with a path to ELD/telematics integrations;
  let's talk about your stack in a pilot."
- **"Will it reduce my downtime?"** → "It helps you decide faster with the info in one place. We'd
  measure the impact together in a pilot."
- **"Is my data safe?"** → "Yes — and for this demo everything shown is fictional sample data."
