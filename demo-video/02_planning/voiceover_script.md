# Voiceover Script — TruckFixr Coolant-Leak Product Demo

Style: confident, conversational, benefit-led, fleet-operations aware. Guiding a fleet owner or
maintenance manager through a real use case. No AI hype, no jargon, no overpromising.
Pace target ≈ 150 wpm. Total ≈ 3:05. Word count ≈ 300.

Read the **[VO]** lines. **(direction)** notes are for the voice talent / TTS, not spoken.
On-screen text is in `onscreen_text_plan.md` and must not be read aloud.

---

**Scene 1 — The problem (0:00)**
[VO] Today, a coolant leak might be reported through a call, a text, a paper inspection, or a photo
buried in a chat thread. Fleet teams aren't the problem — they work hard. The problem is that the
information you need to make a maintenance decision ends up scattered.
*(beat)*

**Scene 2 — Introduce TruckFixr (0:20)**
[VO] TruckFixr Fleet AI helps small and mid-sized fleets turn inspections, driver reports, symptoms,
photos, fault codes, and repair history into faster maintenance decisions.

**Scene 3 — Fleet dashboard (0:35)**
[VO] We'll start in the fleet dashboard. Here a manager can see vehicles, open issues, inspection
activity, and maintenance priorities in one place — instead of chasing them across tools.

**Scene 4 — Driver reports the leak (0:55)**
[VO] In this example, the driver notices a low-coolant warning during pre-trip and sees coolant near
the reservoir. Instead of a loose text or a call that loses context, the driver submits it straight
into TruckFixr — the vehicle, the symptom, a note, and a photo, all captured together.

**Scene 5 — AI-assisted triage (1:25)**
[VO] TruckFixr organizes the report and runs AI-assisted triage. Here it's flagged high priority —
a coolant leak can create overheating risk if the truck is dispatched without inspection — with
suggested next steps: check the reservoir, cap, hoses and clamps, and pressure-test the cooling
system.
*(direction: land on "high priority" — it's the turning point)*

**Scene 6 — Manager reviews context (1:55)**
[VO] Before deciding, the manager reviews the vehicle's history, recent maintenance, inspection
status, and upcoming compliance. There was a coolant reservoir repair before, a preventive service
just eighteen days ago, and an annual inspection due in about six weeks. The issue is no longer just
a message — it's part of the vehicle record.

**Scene 7 — The decision (2:25)**
[VO] With the report, the triage, the history, and the inspection context all together, the manager
can make a faster call: hold the truck, schedule the repair, and assign a backup unit — before it's
dispatched, and before it becomes a roadside problem.

**Scene 8 — Partner pathway (2:45)**
[VO] TruckFixr is live today and available for pilot conversations with small and mid-sized fleets
and partners. And it's built with a path to ELD, telematics, fault-code, and repair-partner
integrations.

**Scene 9 — CTA (3:00)**
[VO] Explore a TruckFixr pilot or partnership.
*(direction: warm, unhurried close)*

## Claims-safe substitution list (if any line is challenged)
- "faster maintenance decisions" ✔ (never "guaranteed less downtime")
- "AI-assisted triage" ✔ (never "the AI diagnoses/decides")
- "suggested next steps" ✔ (never "the fix")
- "built with a path to … integrations" ✔ (never "integrates with")
- "available for pilot conversations" ✔ (never "trusted by X fleets" unless documented)

## Timing notes for the silent/captioned cut
Every VO sentence maps to one caption card (see onscreen_text_plan.md). The silent version must be
fully understandable from those cards alone.
