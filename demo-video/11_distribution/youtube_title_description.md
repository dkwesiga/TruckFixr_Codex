## Description
TruckFixr Fleet AI helps small and mid-sized fleets turn inspections, driver reports, symptoms,
photos, fault codes, and repair history into faster maintenance decisions.

In this 3-minute product demo, follow one real workflow: a driver spots a low-coolant warning during
pre-trip and reports it straight into TruckFixr instead of a scattered call, text, or photo thread.
TruckFixr organizes the report, AI-assisted triage flags it as high priority for overheating risk
with suggested next steps, and the fleet manager reviews the vehicle's history and compliance
context — then makes the call before dispatch: hold the truck, schedule the repair, assign a backup
unit.
