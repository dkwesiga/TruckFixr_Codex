A coolant leak spotted at pre-trip shouldn't turn into roadside downtime.

But too often the information needed to make the call — the driver's report, the symptom, a photo,
the vehicle's history, the compliance status — is scattered across calls, texts, and paper forms. So
the decision waits.

We built TruckFixr Fleet AI to close that gap for small and mid-sized fleets.

Here's the workflow in our new 3-minute demo:
→ A driver reports a low-coolant warning straight into TruckFixr — structured, not a loose text
→ AI-assisted triage flags it high priority for overheating risk, with suggested next steps
→ The manager reviews history + compliance in one record
→ The call gets made before dispatch: hold the truck, schedule the repair, assign a backup unit

TruckFixr helps fleets turn inspections, driver reports, fault codes, photos, and repair history
into faster maintenance decisions. It's live today and available for pilot conversations — and built
with a path to ELD, telematics, fault-code, and repair-partner integrations.

If you run a fleet or work in fleet maintenance, I'd value your take. Open to pilot and partner
conversations.
